# E0 on nobara, amendment C3: five models resident on the RTX 2070 SUPER, 2026-10-18

Amendment C3 (`e1-calibration-preregistration-2026-10-18.md` §9) ran E0 on the five models that load `cuda-resident` on
nobara's 8 GB card at a measured context. Every result is under E0 amendment 9's probes. It is a table for the owner,
not a decision: picking a model for calibration is a separate amendment.

## What ran

- **When:** 14:14:30–15:40:25 PDT, by day on the owner's override. One hold of nobara's timing lock covered all five.
  The script is `~/tack-runs/e0/c3-2026-10-18.sh` and its log `~/tack-runs/e0/c3-2026-10-18.log`. Each model's
  results are in `~/tack-runs/e0/c3-2026-10-18/<arm>/results.jsonl`.
- **Builds:**
  - goinfer-serve `f20f0d93` (sha256 `b00ed6183e2610a9…`) for all five. Every pre-run load was `cuda-resident` at its
    context, so the 0de92cd9 fallback was never used.
  - tack `ed2725c` (sha256 `daab07a1f042ef0a…`).
- **Thinking:** off for the four Qwen models. Server flag `-thinking off`, and tack sends thinking disabled. Granite's
  thinking is unmanaged. Every probe streamed text only: `first_kind` was text, and no thinking characters streamed.
- **The order** was the owner's. All five ran.

## What each result means

- **Qwen3.5-9B passes the gate, but its prefix is never reused.**
  - Its first-token time with the prefix reused equals the cold time at every size: 15.3 s against 15.7 s at 4k, and
    421.3 s against 421.5 s at 31,744.
  - So goinfer re-prefilled the whole prompt every time. Its cold prefill is also slow: about 260 tok/s at 4k, and
    75 tok/s at 31k.
  - tack's design depends on prefix reuse: the context only grows at its tail between compactions. A turn of 15
    requests at 20k tokens would spend tens of minutes in prefill.
  - Qwen3.5 interleaves linear-attention (gated DeltaNet) layers with full attention. Whether goinfer can reuse a prefix
    for them on CUDA is goinfer's question, and is not established here.
- **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B is void.** goinfer's swap guard tripped in E0's check phase ("swap grew past the guard threshold;
  refusing new requests"), and every later request got a 503. Host RSS peaked at 33.1 GB. With its experts streamed
  from host memory, it does not run E0 on nobara.
- **Qwen3-8B passes**, at 19/20 on both runs, with 0 malformed. Its prefix reuse works: 1.9 s to first token at 15k
  with the prefix reused, against 26.7 s cold. It decodes at 79 tok/s. Its context is 16,384, under the calibration's
  registered 32,768.
- **Qwen3-4B passes**, at 18/20 on both runs, with 0 malformed. Prefix reuse works (1.9 s at 29.7k), it decodes at
  130 tok/s, and its context is 30,720.
- **Granite-4.2-3B fails** at 6/20 on both runs. It answered 11 of the 20 prompts without a tool call, and 3 with glob
  where grep was wanted. It has no malformed calls. It is the fastest: 152 tok/s.
- **Every smoke run matched its twin exactly:** no task differed between run 1 and run 2 for any model. The misses
  shared by the Qwen models are E0's task 1, where each globbed without `_test.go`, and, for Qwen3-4B, task 13.

**Against the predictions recorded in §9:**

- Qwen3.5-9B: smoke 19 (predicted 17–19), decode 65 (50–75), cold first token at 15k not measured. The 31.7k cold time
  of 421 s is far past the 2–8 s predicted for 15k.
- Qwen3.6-35B-A3B: predicted to pass. It is void.
- Qwen3-8B: smoke 19 (predicted 16–18, "on the edge"), decode 79 (65–85), cold first token at 15k 26.7 s (2–6 s).
- Qwen3-4B: smoke 18 (14–17, "likely below"), decode 130 (90–130).
- Granite-4.2-3B: smoke 6 (12–16), decode 152 (90–140).

The cold first-token times are well above the predictions, which were too optimistic. The reused times are what an
A1 turn mostly pays.
