**Time to first token, ms, three repeats each:**

| prompt | cold | append (the gate) | rewrite (secondary, outside the gate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4,096 | 16,136–16,336, median 16,188 | 2,852–5,313, median 5,247 | 4,619 / 4,981 / 15,938 |
| 8,192 | 39,699–40,125, median 39,912 | 5,734–9,846, median 6,147 | 5,751–5,849, median 5,789 |
| 31,744 | 279,995–280,584, median 280,333 | 10,611–19,583, median 18,879 | 16,715 / 278,634 / 279,891 |

- **The append cell reuses the prefix.** C3's reused cell, a rewrite on `f20f0d93`, equaled the cold time at every
  size. Here appends run at 0.07–0.32 of cold.
- **The rewrite is mixed.** At 8k all three repeats reused. At 4k and 31,744, some repeats came back at the cold
  time: the third at 4k, and the second and third at 31,744. The rewrite is outside the gate (amendment 11); it is
  reported, not read.
- **Cold prefill is faster than C3's at the top cell:** 280 s against 421 s at 31,744. At 4k and 8k it is about the
  same: 16.2 s against 15.7 s, and 39.9 s against 38.8 s.
- **Appends are not cheap.** The appended text is the previous reply plus 512 tokens, yet it costs 5–19 s, rising
  with the prefix. Qwen3-8B reused at 15k in 1.9 s (C3). That matters for step 2's timing, not for the gate.
