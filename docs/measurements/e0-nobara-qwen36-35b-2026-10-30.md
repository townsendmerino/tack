## Why 16,384, not the 32,768 of nobara-big01's serve check

§14.1 registered the window as 16,384 before goinfer's serve command was posted. nobara-big01 served the model at 32,768;
this E0 and §14.2's runs use 16,384.
- **It is C6's window, and tack's design window** (DESIGN §1; CLAUDE.md, "Small window first"). §14.2's runs are read
  beside §12.21's B (Qwen3-8B at 16,384), and a different window would confound the model with the window, as C5's
  record found for 16k against 32k.
- **It keeps E0 short:** its top timed cell is 15,360, not 31,744, which nobara-big01 measured at about 6 minutes per
  cold request.
- **E0 admits the model only at that window.** Whether it holds 32,768 under E0 is not measured here.
